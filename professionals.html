03The sprint

Four weeks. Every one builds on the last.

Intensive by design. You leave week four with a deployed workflow, applied deliverables and a defended capstone, not a certificate of attendance.

Week 01

Foundation & mapping

Map the AI landscape in your field, audit your current tools, and find the highest-leverage opportunities in your specific role. Scope your project.

Week 02

Applied build

Hands-on deployment in live work scenarios, guided by your practitioner. First deliverable built, reviewed, and ready to use in your actual role.

Week 03

Depth & judgement

Where the tools break, what to verify before you trust an output, and which parts of your work should never be automated at all.

Week 04

Capstone & defence

Ship your project against a real workflow, then defend it live to your practitioner and your cohort. Certificate issued on completion.

04The framework

Three pillars. One transformation.

Develop all three and you do not just know how to use AI. You become the professional others in your field follow.

01

Capacity

Field-specific fluency, not general AI literacy. Which tools to reach for, when, and how to read their output in your professional context.

02

Character

The judgement to deploy AI accountably. Every field creates decisions a model should not make alone, knowing where that line sits is the skill.

03

Cadence

The habit of applying it week after week, not once in a workshop. Built through a cohort on the same problems and a practitioner holding the pace.
