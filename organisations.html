07The deliverable

The report your leadership actually reads

Most training ends with an attendance spreadsheet. This is the document that goes to whoever signed the budget, and it includes the parts that did not work.

  • Capability movement per competency: where the team started, where it landed, measured against the same rubric both times.
  • Every applied project, listed: what was built, against which workflow, and whether it survived its defence.
  • What we would not automate: the workflows where we think AI would cost you accuracy or trust. Usually the most useful page.
  • A 90-day next-phase recommendation, which functions to deploy into next, and which to leave alone for now.
