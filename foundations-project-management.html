03Who it is for

04The twelve weeks

Plan it, run it when it goes wrong, then prove what happened.

You plan a real project in the first phase, and the cohort runs it in the second. We introduce the problems deliberately, because a plan that never meets resistance teaches nobody anything.

01
Weeks 1 — 4

Planning that survives

Most plans fail because they assume everything goes right. You learn to build one that already expects not to.

  • Breaking work down until estimates mean something
  • Dependencies, and which ones actually matter
  • Risks written down before they happen
  • Agreeing what done means, in writing
02
Weeks 5 — 8

When it goes wrong

The core of the course. Things slip on purpose and you decide what happens next, with people watching.

  • Re-planning without restarting everything
  • Telling someone senior that it will be late
  • Chasing people without becoming the problem
  • Deciding what to drop, and defending it
03
Weeks 9 — 12

Reporting, and AI

The admin that eats a project manager's week is exactly the kind of work worth automating, so we do.

  • Status reports that get read
  • AI for the updates, summaries and chasing
  • Where automated tracking misleads you
  • Closing properly, including what went badly
