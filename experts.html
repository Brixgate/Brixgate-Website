01Why practitioners

A trainer can explain a tool. A practitioner knows when not to use it.

The difference shows up in the questions nobody prepares for: the ones that start "but in our case…". That is the part we hire for.

They have shipped it, not just read it

Every expert has deployed AI inside the profession they teach, and can show what it changed. Teaching comes second to having done the work.

They will tell you what not to automate

The most valuable sessions are the ones where an expert says a workflow should stay human. You cannot get that from a course that is selling AI.

Their name is on your certificate

The credential carries the assessment of the practitioner who reviewed your capstone. That is a person putting their reputation behind your work.

03Matching

How you get paired with yours

You are not assigned whoever is free. Matching runs on the field assessment you complete before the cohort starts.

01

You complete a field assessment

A short diagnostic on where you actually sit: your role, your tools, and the workflows you want to change. It takes minutes, not hours.

02

We match on field, not availability

You are paired with a practitioner from your profession whose applied experience lines up with the problems you brought.

03

They review and sign your capstone

The same expert who taught you assesses what you built and writes the assessment that appears on your verifiable certificate.
