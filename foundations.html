Who this is for

Being honest about it saves everyone time.

Foundations is deliberately slow at the start. If you already work in a field and want AI inside that job, this is the wrong door and you will be bored by week three.

This is you

Foundations fits

  • You have used a chatbot but could not explain what it is actually doing.
  • You are changing careers, or have not started one yet.
  • You keep starting free tutorials and losing the thread halfway through.
  • You would rather be taught by a person than left with a video library.
This is not you

Look at Professionals instead

  • You are already paid to do this work and have been for a while.
  • You want AI applied to your existing job, not taught from scratch.
  • You have real work you want to bring into the room in week one.
  • Four focused weeks suits you better than twelve paced ones.
Genuinely unsure? The free readiness check takes about three minutes and tells you which track fits where you actually are. You do not need to give an email to see the result.
The shape of it

Three phases across twelve weeks.

Longer than the professional sprints on purpose. Building the ground floor properly is what makes everything after it hold up.

01

Ground floor

Weeks 1 to 4

What these systems are, what they are not, and why they behave the way they do. You finish able to explain the thing you have been using, and to spot when it is wrong.

02

Hands on the tools

Weeks 5 to 8

Working properly rather than guessing. Structured practice on real tasks, with the habits that separate someone getting lucky from someone who knows what they are doing.

03

Build something real

Weeks 9 to 12

A project taken from nothing to finished, with the reasoning documented. It is the piece you show people afterwards, and the bridge into a professional track.
