Starting out

What the first month actually looks like.

The hardest part of starting is not knowing what you are walking into. So here it is, week by week, before you commit to anything.

01Before it starts

You do not have to prepare anything.

There is no reading list and no setup homework. We send you a joining link, the times, and one short note about what to expect. That is deliberately all, because people who feel behind before week one tend not to make it to week three.

If you are worried about the tech: the only thing you need is a laptop and a stable enough connection to hold a video call.
02Week one

Everyone in the room is where you are.

The first session is spent making sure nobody is lost, which is slower than a lot of courses and on purpose. You will be asked what you already use and what confuses you, and the answers shape how the next few weeks are taught.

Nobody is put on the spot. You can stay on mute for the whole of week one if that is what you need.
03When you get stuck

Getting stuck is the method, not the failure.

You will hit something that does not make sense. Everyone does. There is a practitioner in the session to ask, a small group who are hitting the same wall, and a channel between sessions that someone actually reads.

If you miss a session it is recorded, and the next one starts by catching up whoever needs it.

Who this is for

Being honest about it saves everyone time.

Foundations is deliberately slow at the start. If you already work in a field and want AI inside that job, this is the wrong door and you will be bored by week three.

This is you

Foundations fits

  • You have used a chatbot but could not explain what it is actually doing.
  • You are changing careers, or have not started one yet.
  • You keep starting free tutorials and losing the thread halfway through.
  • You would rather be taught by a person than left with a video library.
This is not you

Look at Professionals instead

  • You are already paid to do this work and have been for a while.
  • You want AI applied to your existing job, not taught from scratch.
  • You have real work you want to bring into the room in week one.
  • Four focused weeks suits you better than twelve paced ones.
Genuinely unsure? The free readiness check takes about three minutes and tells you which track fits where you actually are. You do not need to give an email to see the result.
