01Who it is for

If your week has a task you dread repeating, this is for you.

It does not matter what you do for a living. What matters is that something in your week is manual, repetitive and predictable, because that is exactly what an automation replaces.

This is you

Six weeks will suit you

  • You copy things between spreadsheets, inboxes or tools by hand every week.
  • You have never written code and would rather not start by learning a language.
  • You want something working quickly rather than a long theoretical grounding.
  • You can find about four hours a week for six weeks.
This is not you

Look elsewhere in Foundations

  • You want the deep theory behind the models. Data Science runs sixteen weeks for that.
  • You are already automating things at work and want to go further.
  • You need a credential for a specific profession rather than a working skill.
02What changes

The same week, six weeks later.

Before
  • Monday morning spent pulling the same numbers into the same report.
  • Replies written from scratch that are ninety percent the same each time.
  • Things missed because nobody remembered to check.
After
  • The report is in your inbox before you open your laptop.
  • Drafts written for you, in your own voice, waiting for a yes or a fix.
  • The check runs whether or not anyone remembers it.

04Worked examples

The kinds of things people build here.

You build your own, but these are the shapes that come up again and again, and we work through each of them together.

Inbox triage

Sort what arrives, draft the reply that is nearly always the same, and flag the one that genuinely needs you.

The weekly report

Pull the numbers, write the summary, send it to the people who need it, on a schedule you set once.

Document handling

Read what comes in, pull out the fields that matter, file it where it belongs and tell someone it arrived.
