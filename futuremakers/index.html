An example, not a real scholarship

The page above shows the layout every named scholarship gets. The name, the message and the family are invented to demonstrate it — the first real ones go up when the first sponsors create them.

You write the name and the message. You choose the programme and how many places. We publish it once Operations has reviewed it, and it stays up with its own link for as long as it runs.

What happens after you pay.

The part most giving goes quiet on.

You choose and pay

Programme, number of learners, and whether the scholarship carries a name. Payment through Paystack or Stripe.

We find the people

Applications open against published criteria. We shortlist and select, or enrol the person you nominated.

They do the work

Live cohorts taught by practitioners, with attendance and progress tracked the same way every Brixgate learner's is.

You find out what happened

Who was selected, how they did, and what they finished with. Not a thank-you note — the actual outcome.

Who the money reaches.

You pick the group. We publish the criteria we select against, so nobody has to take the selection on trust.

Students

In school or just out, with no route into technical work and no money for one.

Youth

Working or looking, old enough to need the income and young enough to change direction.

Adults

Mid-career, changing field, or returning to work after time away.
