Model-agnostic by design

We don't teach one tool.
We teach judgement.

Models change every few months. The practitioners who last are the ones who can pick the right model for the task, check its work, and know when not to use it at all.

Pick the right model

Reasoning, speed and cost differ sharply between models. You learn to match the model to the job instead of defaulting to whichever one you opened first.

Verify before you ship

AI fails quietly and confidently. Every sprint drills the habit of checking output against the standards of your own profession before it reaches anyone else.

Know the limits

Confidentiality, bias, hallucination, accountability. Field-specific judgement about where AI has no business being is as valuable as knowing how to prompt it.
