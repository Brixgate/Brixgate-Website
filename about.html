What we do

Two engines, one practitioner network.

The people we train are the people who do the transformation work. That is the whole design: the second engine runs on the first.

Engine one
We train the practitioners

AI in my Field is a four week sprint for people already doing the work, taught by a practitioner from that exact field. AI Foundations runs six to sixteen weeks for people starting from nothing. Both end in work you defend and a certificate anyone can verify.

See the programmes

Engine two
We rebuild how institutions run

Brixgate Transform is a four phase engagement — diagnose, design, deploy, institutionalize — that changes the operating model rather than the tool list. Governance is written in: a named person owns every decision AI touches, and client data stays in the client's environment.

See Brixgate Transform

How we judge it

Capacity. Character. Cadence.

Three pillars, and all three have to be there. Develop one and you have a person who knows the tools. Develop all three and you have a practitioner others in the field follow.

01
Capacity

Fluency in your own field, not general AI literacy. Which tools to reach for, when, and how to read their output in your professional context.

02
Character

The judgement to deploy AI accountably. Every field creates decisions a model should not make alone, and knowing where that line sits is the skill.

03
Cadence

The habit of applying it week after week, not once in a workshop. Built through a cohort on the same problems and a practitioner holding the pace.
