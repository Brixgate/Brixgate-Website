Before you choose

Nobody starts out knowing the difference.

So here it is, plainly. Every product has a part people see and touch, and a part that stores the data and does the work. You can learn either one, or both. None of the three assumes you have written code before.

Track one Frontend The part people actually touch. Screens, buttons, the thing that has to work on a phone on a bad connection. Pick this if you are visual, and you want to see what you built the moment you build it. Track two Backend Everything behind the screen. Where the data lives, how it moves, and what happens when ten thousand people arrive at once. Pick this if you like systems and logic, and the problems nobody sees. Track three Fullstack Both ends, and the seam between them — which is where most of the interesting problems live. The longest of the three, on purpose. Pick this if you want to carry a product from idea to shipped on your own.
